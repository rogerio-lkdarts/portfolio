Hi, I'm Rogério!

At 26 years old, and with a career path that began in 2018, I am embarking on a new professional chapter focused on my active return to the technology sector. I am a software developer with solid experience in the financial and banking industry, specializing in modernizing undocumented, monolithic legacy systems—transforming them into scalable architectures designed to reduce infrastructure costs, minimize technical support needs, and enhance operational efficiency. By leveraging AI-assisted development, I have managed to reduce project delivery times and costs by up to three times.

With over 5 years of development experience, more than 2 years in Generative AI, and over 1 year in data engineering, I have managed mission-critical systems for organizations with revenues in the millions or billions of dollars—consistently ensuring high availability, regulatory compliance, and adherence to mandatory requirements.

I place great value on understanding the underlying processes, as this fosters better collaboration across teams and enables the creation of seamless user experiences that drive both results and revenue.
